## RESEARCH CHALLENGES & LEARNINGS

### Major Challenges:
1. **Limited web presence for distributors** - Many B2B companies don't maintain public websites
2. **Brand confidentiality** - Distributor partnerships often kept private
3. **Regional variations** - Asian/African companies have lower online presence
4. **Access restrictions** - Some websites block automated scraping
5. **Name variations** - Company names may differ across databases

### Best Sources Found:
1. Company official websites and "About" pages
2. LinkedIn company pages (when public)
3. Government business registries (UK Companies House, Malaysia CTOS, etc.)
4. Business intelligence platforms (PitchBook, Crunchbase, Tracxn)
5. Industry news and press releases
6. Financial databases (Yahoo Finance for public companies)

### Lessons:
1. Older companies (1970s-1990s) often have better documented history
2. Public companies provide more accessible financial and employee data
3. Private distributors rarely disclose brand partnerships publicly
4. Asian companies less likely to have English-language web presence
5. Direct company contact often necessary for full data accuracy
